# Limitations

What this approach cannot do, what cannot be known, and what has already been tried.

**[What We Cannot Answer](unanswerable.md)** -- Questions that aren't answerable from public sources, most of them about how real cross-domain guards actually behave. Six of seven things you'd need to design a transfer format are controlled information. Designing against a guess is worse than not designing.

**[What Has Failed Before](history.md)** -- The idea has failed four times since 1968, and the diagnoses are consistent and unflattering. One of them contains the best positioning argument available.

**[Ecosystem Health](ecosystem.md)** -- Twelve projects under 300 stars on the critical path, a graveyard of famous-but-dead tools, and a licence pattern where the paid tier is reliably the feature a regulated deployment needs.
